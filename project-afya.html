MDH Afya Jumuishi Projects

Project Description

Afya Jumuishi is a project implemented by Management and Development for Health (MDH), funded by CDC, aimed at supporting the Government of Tanzania to provide comprehensive HIV and TB prevention, care and treatment services. The project is implemented across three regions of Dar es Salaam, Kagera, and Tabora, in collaboration with the Ministry of Health (MOH), the President’s Office of Regional Administration and Local Government (PORALG), and the corresponding Regional and Council Health Management Teams (R/CHMTs). The project supports 573 health facilities and 443 wards across all 21 councils in these three regions.

The Afya Jumuishi Project contributes to Tanzania’s mission to end AIDS as a public health threat by 2030 in line with the UNAIDS 2030 agenda. Supported services include:

  • HIV Testing and Prevention Services, which focuses on ensuring that all people living with HIV know their HIV status and are linked to lifesaving anti-retroviral treatment. Individuals who are HIV-negative but at risk of HIV infection are offered effective HIV prevention services for as long as needed to keep them free from the infection.
  • Comprehensive HIV Care and Treatment focuses on ensuring that all people living with HIV, also known as recipients of care (ROCs), are provided with and sustained on effective Anti-Retroviral Treatment (ART) and supported to adhere to and remain on lifelong treatment.
  • Health System Strengthening focuses on contributing to ensuring that core components of the healthcare system, such as Laboratory services, Supply Chain Management, Health Information Systems, Human Resources for Health (HRH), and others, are well equipped to support comprehensive HIV and other healthcare services.

    • My Role & Approach

    As a Strategic Information Officer at MDH, I led a team of Data Officers in ensuring high-quality data management and reporting across program activities. My responsibilities included overseeing data collection processes, ensuring accurate and timely data entry, preparing routine and ad hoc reports, and providing technical support through computer troubleshooting. I conducted training, mentorship, and supportive supervision to strengthen staff capacity, monitored program performance and progress against key indicators, and ensured data collection tools and reporting systems were regularly updated. I also played a key role in data quality assurance through systematic data cleaning, verification, and triangulation processes, while supporting indicator interpretation, data analysis, and the use of evidence for informed decision-making and program improvement.

    Deriverables & Results
  • Translated program data into research outputs, lessons learned, success stories, and manuscript contributions, strengthening evidence use, reporting quality, and program learning.
  • Contributed to the development of MDH strategic department job aids, guidelines, and SOPs, supporting standardization of data management practices.
  • Supervised and provided technical oversight to 120+ data officers, ensuring quality and consistency in daily data management activities.
  • Conducted 10 DQAs at facility level, identifying gaps and implementing corrective actions in collaboration with CHMT and RHMT which Improved data quality and reporting.
  • Managed weekly data collection, cleaning, transformation, and analysis, generating actionable insights to inform program improvements.
  • Coordinated monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and annual data collection, analysis, and reporting processes, ensuring high-quality datasets for program performance review and donor reporting
  • Designed and developed interactive dashboards and visual reports, enabling efficient tracking of key indicators and improving data accessibility for decision-makers
