About me

Who am I? - The data hero

My journey into data analysis has never been just about mastering software, it has always been driven by a passion for solving problems. Throughout my career, I have seen organizations and projects work tirelessly while losing valuable time struggling with messy and disorganised data. I discovered that my greatest strength lies in transforming data chaos into clean, structured, and actionable information. For me, data is more than numbers, it is a tool for creating clarity, empowering teams, and supporting better decision-making. I enjoy building smart, practical solutions that help organizations save time, improve performance, and achieve meaningful real world impact.

What I do best - My superpowers

I handle the entire lifecycle of data, turning chaotic information into clear strategy. I design robust MEL systems to measure real world project impact, build clean surveys from scratch, and tame messy datasets including handwritten registries into flawless databases you can trust. Ultimately, I bridge the gap between deep statistical analysis and intuitive dashboards so leaders can see the truth of their data at a single glance.

My Tool kits - My weapons

For questionnaires design and data capturing, I used KoboCollect. For data extraction, transformation, and database querying, I use SQL to clean, structure, and retrieve complex datasets with precision. For deep-dive analysis, R and STATA are my statistical powerhouses, while Advanced Excel remains my trusty sidekick for quick, elegant workflows. When insights need a geographic lens, I use QGIS for spatial mapping. For visual storytelling, I build dynamic dashboards in Power BI, using Power Query, M code, and DAX to shape and model complex data behind the scenes. And as a next frontier, I am sharpening my Python skills to bring even more automation to my arsenal.

Outside data - The secret identity

When I amm not in data-taming mode, you will usually find me exploring new business ideas, keeping an eye on the latest in automotive engineering, or looking for my next big learning challenge. I love diving into a good thriller or sci-fi novel, but I am just as likely to be reading up on finance, psychology, or losing myself in a YouTube rabbit hole about ancient history and great leaders of the past.
