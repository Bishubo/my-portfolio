About me

Who am I? - The data hero

My journey into data analysis is driven by a passion for solving problems. I enjoy transforming complex and disorganised data into clear, actionable insights that help organizations save time, improve performance, and make better decisions.

My Tool kits - My weapons

My toolkit covers the entire data workflow: KoboCollect for data collection, SQL for data management, R and STATA for statistical analysis, Power BI for dashboards, QGIS for spatial analysis, and Advanced Excel for everyday data tasks. I'm also expanding my skills in Python for automation and advanced analytics.

What I do best - My superpowers

I design MEL Systems (Logical Frameworks, MEL Plan, Indicator Tracking Tables etc.), Survey design (Baseline, Midline, Endline), Desining tools and conduction data collection (Quaitative & Quantitative), clean and manage complex datasets, perform statistical analysis, report preparation and disemination of results, conduct training and mentorship and build dashboards that turn data into actionable insights.

Outside data - The secret identity

Outside of data, I explore business ideas, automotive engineering, and continuous learning opportunities. I’m particularly interested in history and psychology, especially how influential figures and leadership decisions shape outcomes. I also enjoy reading thrillers and science fiction, alongside topics like finance and human behavior.
