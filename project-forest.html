Hillside Forest Restoration and Regreening the Surrounding Landscape

Project Description

Butiama Tanzania: Hillside forest restoration and regreening the surrounding landscape, Forest restoration in the Butiama District focused on nurturing back forest on the hillsides and increasing the tree components in the wider landscape. This program also involved the creation of community forests of these hillsides with a legal status and to improve the water catchment and as such dried up water springs. Forest restoration will be done by assisted natural regeneration and the introduction of individual woodlots and agroforestry systems that will improve the livelihoods of the local population.

This project was funded by WeForest and impremented by Global Resource Alliance. The total area of intervention is 2,057 ha over 8 years of which 1,275 ha of hillsides and 782 ha of Agroforestry Systems fall within the boundaries of the Busegwe, Kigori, Mwikoro, and Nyanza villages with a total estimate of up to 3,114,300 trees to be planted.

The key objectives of the project was:

  • Enhance forest governance
  • Increase tree & forest cover
  • Improve woody resources, food security and income of small holder farmers

My role in the project

As the Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) Coordinator at GRA, I was responsible for designing, managing, and strengthening the project’s MEL systems throughout the entire project lifecycle. I developed key M&E tools and frameworks including Logic Models, MLE Plans, Indicator Tracking Tables (ITT), data collection tools, and Activity Tracking Sheets to support effective monitoring, reporting, and learning.

I led the design and implementation of baseline, midline, endline, and other assessment surveys, using mobile data collection platforms such as ODK and KoBoCollect to improve data accuracy, efficiency, and real-time reporting. This helped transition field operations from paper-based processes into organized digital data systems.
My role also involved managing data quality assurance processes through routine data verification, validation exercises, supportive supervision, and Data Quality Assessments (DQAs) to ensure reliability and consistency of project data. I coordinated M&E budgeting, reporting timelines, indicator performance tracking, and compliance with donor and organizational reporting requirements.

In addition, I provided technical support and capacity building to field teams, partners, and data collectors through training, mentorship, and continuous guidance on data collection, reporting procedures, and the use of digital tools.
I worked extensively with Excel, Power BI, GIS, SQL, R and statistical analysis techniques to clean, analyze, visualize, and interpret large datasets. I also developed interactive dashboards, automated reporting systems, maps, and data visualization tools that improved data accessibility and supported timely decision-making.
Working closely with program teams, project leadership, donors, and government stakeholders, I translated complex statistical and spatial data into practical insights that informed planning, performance monitoring, resource allocation, and evidence-based decision-making.

Key Deliverables
  • Translated the Butiama Hillside project Theory of change into comprehensive Logical framework, strengthening results-based planning and performance tracking.
  • Designed and operationalized a robust M&E system, including MEL plan, indicators, and activity tracking tools, improving program monitoring efficiency and data utilization
  • Developed and deployed 20+ digital data collection tools in Kobo Collect with advanced logic and skip patterns, significantly enhancing data accuracy and reducing inconsistencies across project sites
  • Led the design and implementation of 9+ surveys (baseline and midline), generating critical evidence to inform program strategy and adaptive management
  • Conducted GIS and geospatial analysis for 735 farmers’ plots in Mara, supporting land-use planning, program targeting, and spatial decision-making.
  • Strengthened community data systems by training and mentoring 17 lead farmers on data collection, reporting, and data use, improving data quality at field level.
  • Automated data workflows by integrating Kobo Collect with Excel and Power BI, reducing manual processing time and enabling near real-time reporting.
  • Built interactive dashboards in Power BI, enabling real-time visualization of program performance and indicator tracking for data-driven decision-making.
